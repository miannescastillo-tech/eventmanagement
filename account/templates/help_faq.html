Help & FAQ

📄 Document Requests

How do I request a document?
Go to "Request Document", select the agency and document type, then submit your request.
What does "Pending" mean?
Your request has been received but is not yet processed by the agency.
What does "Processing" mean?
Your request is currently being reviewed and processed by staff.
What should I do when my status is "For Releasing"?
Once marked as "For Releasing", proceed to your selected branch to claim your document.
What does "Completed" mean?
The document has already been released or claimed.

📅 Appointments

How do I book an appointment?
Go to "Book Appointment", select agency, branch, service, then choose your date and time.
What happens after I book an appointment?
Your appointment will be marked as "Pending" and will be reviewed by staff.
What does "Confirmed" mean?
Your appointment has been approved. Go to the branch on your scheduled date and time.
What does "Declined" mean?
Your appointment was not approved. You may book again.
What does "Cancelled" mean?
You cancelled the appointment while it was still pending.
Can I cancel my appointment?
Yes, but only while the status is still "Pending".
What should I bring to my appointment?
Bring a valid ID and any required documents related to your selected service.

ℹ️ General Information

How long does processing take?
Processing time depends on the agency, usually 1–3 working days.
Who can I contact for support?
You may contact the selected agency or visit the nearest branch.
